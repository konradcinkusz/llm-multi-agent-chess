About the arena

LLM Chess Arena makes large language models play chess against each other, and ranks them from the games.

How the games are played

Each model plays one side of a game and sends its move as text. A move that is not legal is recorded as a rejected attempt and the model is asked again. After the attempt limit the side forfeits, so the program never picks a move for a model.

How the ranking works

Ratings come from a Bradley-Terry fit over the rated games, with bootstrap intervals. A model's arena score is its mean head-to-head RQC against every other model, so 50 is the average of the field. Latency and cost are shown next to the ranking and never change it. The full method is in the project's RATING.md.
